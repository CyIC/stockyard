## 1. Summary

Stockyard v1.0 turns Warren Bot into a **stock research library with two front ends**: the existing
Discord bot (still called **Warren**) and a new **command-line interface** whose primary consumer is an
AI agent that needs the same numbers without spending LLM tokens to compute them. A third front end, an
**MCP server**, follows in v1.1 on the same core.

The core produces the BetterInvesting **Stock Selection Guide (SSG)** analysis as *typed, structured
results*. Front ends never compute; they call the core and hand its results to a renderer (Discord
markdown, terminal text, or JSON).

## 2. Goals and non-goals

**Goals (v1.0)**

1. A pure core library: fetch → align → analyse → typed results. No Discord, no printing, no hidden
   file writes, no dependence on the wall clock.
2. A CLI for humans (text) and agents (`--json`, versioned schema, stable exit codes).
3. The Discord bot ported onto the core with today's commands (`!stock_report`/`!sr`, `!help`).
4. **Issue #5 / ADR-0001 (amended):** every year-over-year comparison reads Aligned Annual Fundamentals
   keyed on the income-statement fiscal period-end.
5. **Issue #2:** short history (including recent IPOs) produces a partial report: each figure has a
   stated minimum (§5.6); below it the figure is `null` with a warning, never a crash.
6. Financial definitions written down (§5.7) and pinned by definition-derived tests.

**Non-goals (v1.0)** — deliberately deferred

| Item | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| MCP server | 1.1 | Thin adapter once the core exists (§9). |
| **Early-stage mode** for new listings | 1.1 | Substitutes TTM/quarterly/since-listing figures where SSG history is missing. New methodology; needs its own design (D13). |
| Graham revised value | 1.1 | New calculation; never implemented anywhere. |
| Issue #4 (issue link in help), `!terms_of_use` | 1.1 | UX only. |
| Club portfolio features | later, redesigned | Removed (ADR-0004). |
| More than ~4 years of annual history (SEC EDGAR backfill) | later | yfinance reliably supplies ~4 annual statements (D17). |
| Semiannual filers | later | 4-quarter completeness rule stands. |

## 5. Core

### 5.1 Data acquisition — `StockData`

* `validate_ticker(raw) -> str`: upper-cases and accepts only `^[A-Z0-9^][A-Z0-9.\-^=]{0,14}$`; anything
  else raises `InvalidTicker` before any network or filesystem use (it also builds cache paths, §7).
* `fetch_stock_data(ticker, *, cache) -> StockData` performs each yfinance call **once** and returns an
  immutable bundle: annual and quarterly income statement, balance sheet and cash flow; quarterly
  earnings (reported EPS with announcement dates) fetched with
  `get_earnings_dates(limit = 4 × annual periods + 12)` (at least 28), enough to reach the first
  quarter of the oldest fiscal year plus the in-progress year and yfinance's scheduled future rows;
  **daily** prices (with per-share dividend amounts) from the start of the oldest annual period covered
  by the income statement (not a fixed `period="5y"`, which misses the oldest fiscal year), or the full
  listing history when no annual statement exists yet. Prices are requested with `auto_adjust=False`:
  split-adjusted but **not dividend-adjusted**. The 0.1 code used yfinance's default `auto_adjust=True`,
  which lowers past prices by every later dividend and so understates historical highs, lows and P/Es
  (most for high-yield stocks); the SSG uses actual traded prices.
* The `_earnings_from_basic` fallback is **removed**: it mapped yfinance `Earnings` (net income) into
  `reportedEPS`, a silent unit error. No reported EPS → EPS-based figures are `null` with a warning.

### 5.2 Aligned Annual Fundamentals (ADR-0001 as amended)

`aligned_annual_fundamentals(data, as_of) -> AlignedFundamentals`.

1. **Fiscal years** are the annual income-statement period-ends `P₁ < … < Pₙ` on or before `as_of`.
2. **Quarter-ends** are *derived*, not read from `quarterly_financials` (which holds only ~5 quarters):
   for each fiscal year `Pᵢ`, the quarter-ends are `Pᵢ − 9 months`, `Pᵢ − 6 months`, `Pᵢ − 3 months`,
   `Pᵢ`. The in-progress year's quarter-ends are `Pₙ + 3`, `Pₙ + 6`, `Pₙ + 9` and `Pₙ + 12 months`, so a
   Q4 release that arrives before yfinance publishes the annual statement still attributes to its own
   quarter. For 52/53-week filers derived quarter-ends are approximate (± 7 days); they are used only to
   bucket announcements.
3. **EPS attribution:** each reported EPS announcement (non-null, announced on or before `as_of`) is
   attributed to the latest derived quarter-end that is strictly before the announcement date and no
   more than 120 days before it. Announcements older than the first derived quarter-end are outside the
   statement history and ignored silently. Because derived quarter-ends are three months apart, a release
   inside the covered span always has one within ~92 days; the 120-day rule therefore fires only past the
   in-progress year (stale annual statements), dropping the release with warning `eps_unattributed`. If two announcements attribute to one quarter-end, the earliest wins and
   `eps_duplicate` is raised.
4. **Fiscal-year rows.** Every annual period-end `Pᵢ` yields a row with revenue and net income from the
   annual statement (the 0.1 "sum of four quarters" revenue fallback is dropped: `quarterly_financials`
   rarely covers a whole older year). `reported_eps` is the sum of the four attributed quarters, or
   `null` if any is missing. A row whose EPS is complete is a **Complete Fiscal Year**.
   Revenue- and income-based figures (Past Sales, Revenue Growth, Earnings Growth) use **all**
   fiscal-year rows; EPS-based figures use **Complete Fiscal Years only**. This keeps pre-IPO S-1 years,
   which have no earnings announcements, in the sales and income history.
5. **Current Year:** the fiscal year after `Pₙ`; carries `quarters_reported` (0–4) and to-date sums. Four
   of four means "awaiting annual statement": shown, labelled, and excluded from comparisons.
6. **`sufficient_history`** is true when at least `YRS_LOOKBACK` (4, D17) Complete Fiscal Years exist.
7. **Balance sheet and cash flow** use only rows whose dates are actual annual period-ends. The current
   code's practice of promoting the latest quarter to a synthetic annual row (stamped `YYYY-12-31`) is
   removed. Cash Position compares the two latest fiscal years and shows the latest quarter separately,
   labelled with its quarter-end.
8. **Fiscal-year price windows:** the price window of fiscal year `Pᵢ` is the daily bars in
   `(Pᵢ₋₁, Pᵢ]` (for the oldest year, the 12 months ending `Pᵢ`). The window is covered when the price
   history starts within 7 days after the window start and ends within 7 days of `Pᵢ` (weekends and
   holidays). A year whose window is not covered (e.g. pre-IPO) has `null` price figures.

### 5.3 Determinism — `as_of`

Every function takes `as_of: date`. Rows dated after `as_of` are ignored. **Present price** is the last
close on or before `as_of`. Look-back windows and the five-year forecast horizon derive from `as_of`.
Front ends pass today's date (America/New_York). Tests pin it. The test fixture loader stops shifting
fixture dates by the current year.

### 5.4 Sections and results

Each section is `section(data, fundamentals, as_of) -> Result`. **No section consumes another section's
result.** Figures needed by more than one section live in shared helpers:
`fiscal_year_price_stats(data, fundamentals, as_of)` returns one row per Complete Fiscal Year (high,
low, EPS, PE at high, PE at low, dividends per share, % payout, high yield), used by Record of Stock and
Risk & Reward alike. `ttm_dividends_per_share(data, as_of)` likewise serves Revenue Growth (yield, Lynch
ratio) and Risk & Reward (dividend support).

| Section id | Result carries |
|---|---|
| `fundamentals` | complete years (`fiscal_year`, `period_end`, `revenue`, `reported_eps`), current year, `sufficient_history`, the `history` block (§5.6) |
| `past_sales` | revenue per FY, recent-pair and oldest-pair averages, % increase, CAGR |
| `past_eps` | reported EPS per FY, pair averages, % increase, CAGR |
| `record_of_stock` | price-stats rows, averages, average PE, present price, TTM EPS, current PE, % vs. high of the oldest FY |
| `trend` | quarterly revenue and EPS series for charts |
| `cash_position` | per metric: latest FY, prior FY, difference, % difference; latest-quarter column |
| `revenue_growth` | revenue per FY with change and % change, average growth, dividends per share, dividend yield, EPS CAGR, current PE, **`lynch_ratio`** |
| `earnings_growth` | net income per FY (income statement, aligned) with change and % change |
| `risk_reward` | forecast high price or the reason none is quoted; four low estimates; zones; up/down ratio per estimate; price target and implied appreciation; forecast series |

`build_report(data, as_of, sections=ALL) -> Report` runs the requested sections and collects warnings.

### 5.5 Failures and warnings

* `InvalidTicker` → CLI exit 2, bot "That doesn't look like a ticker".
* `DataUnavailable` (no statements and no prices) → CLI exit 3; bot "No data available for X".
* `UpstreamError` → CLI exit 4. **Limitation:** yfinance often logs a network failure and returns empty
  frames instead of raising; such failures surface as `DataUnavailable`. Exit 4 is guaranteed only for
  errors yfinance raises.
* A section that raises unexpectedly: its key is present with value `null` and a `section_failed`
  warning; other sections are unaffected.
* Warning codes in v1: `insufficient_history`, `figure_unavailable`, `eps_unattributed`,
  `eps_duplicate`, `non_positive_eps`, `awaiting_annual_statement`, `assumed_zero`, `range_inverted`,
  `price_above_range`, `section_failed`. Each warning has `code`, `section` (or `null`),
  `figure` (or `null`), `message`, and for history warnings `needed` and `available`.

### 5.6 Short history and IPOs (issue #2, D13)

Each figure computes from what exists and states its minimum. Below the minimum it is `null` with a
`figure_unavailable` warning naming what was needed and what was available.

| Figure | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Sales / net income per FY, their year-over-year growth | 2 fiscal-year rows |
| Sales CAGR, sales pair % increase | 2 / 4 fiscal-year rows |
| EPS per FY, EPS growth | 2 Complete Fiscal Years |
| EPS CAGR | 2 Complete Fiscal Years (n − 1 periods) |
| EPS pair % increase | 4 Complete Fiscal Years |
| Record of Stock rows | fiscal years whose price window is fully covered |
| Present price | 1 close on or before `as_of` |
| TTM EPS, current PE | 4 reported quarters |
| TTM dividends per share, dividend yield | 1 close and 365 days of price history before `as_of` (a non-payer has 0, not `null`) |
| Lynch ratio | EPS CAGR, current PE and dividend yield all available |
| Cash Position FY columns | 2 annual balance sheets (latest-quarter column needs 1 quarterly sheet) |
| 5-year forecast high price | sufficient history (4 Complete Fiscal Years, D19), a positive P/E history and ≥ 2 TTM EPS points (≥ 5 quarters) |
| Each low estimate | its own input, independently |
| Zones, up/down ratios, price target | forecast high and the respective low, with low < forecast high |

Every report carries `history`: `complete_fiscal_years`, `quarters_reported`, `first_price_date`,
`sufficient_history`, `price_history_start`. An IPO-day report therefore has sales and net-income
history (often pre-IPO years from the S-1), the trend, present price, and cash position (fiscal-year
columns when the S-1 supplied two annual balance sheets, otherwise only the latest quarter); EPS-based
figures, forecast and zones are `null` with reasons. Substitute
early-stage figures arrive in 1.1.

### 5.7 Financial definitions

All percentages are stored as fractions (`0.12` = 12 %); multiples (PE, up/down ratio, Lynch ratio) are
plain numbers. Any result that is undefined, `NaN` or `±∞` is `null` with a warning.

| Figure | Definition |
|---|---|
| CAGR | `(last / first)^(1/(n−1)) − 1` over n Complete Fiscal Years; `null` + warning if `first ≤ 0` or `last < 0` |
| Pair % increase | `(mean(last two) − mean(first two)) / mean(first two)`; `null` if the base ≤ 0 |
| TTM EPS | sum of the last four reported quarterly EPS on or before `as_of` |
| Current PE | present price / TTM EPS; `null` if TTM EPS ≤ 0 |
| PE at high / low | FY high / FY EPS, FY low / FY EPS; years with EPS ≤ 0 are excluded from averages (`non_positive_eps`) |
| % payout, high yield | dividends per share / EPS; dividends per share / FY low |
| Forecast high price | average PE at high × projected TTM EPS five years after `as_of` (linear fit of TTM EPS). Labelled "Forecast high (avg PE × projected EPS)"; the 0.1 label "Linear Regression of Highs" was wrong. No forecast when projected EPS ≤ 0. |
| Low estimates | The **price window** is the daily bars in the five years up to `as_of`. (a) linear-regression low: linear fit of the window's daily lows, projected five years after `as_of`; **(b) average low: mean of the fiscal-year lows from the price-stats rows** (D14; 0.1 averaged ~1,250 daily lows); (c) severe low: lowest daily low in the window; (d) dividend support: TTM dividends per share / highest fiscal-year high yield |
| Zones (D14) | range = forecast high − chosen low. **Buy:** low → low + 25 %; **Maybe:** middle 50 %; **Sell:** top 25 %. Present price is reported as `buy`, `maybe`, `sell`, `below_range` or `above_range`. If the low ≥ forecast high, zones are `null` with `range_inverted`. |
| Up/down ratio | `(forecast high − present price) / (present price − low)`; `null` with `figure_unavailable` if present price ≤ low; `null` with `price_above_range` if present price ≥ forecast high |
| Price target appreciation | forecast high / present price − 1 (negative when the price is above the forecast high) |
| Dividends per share (fiscal year) | sum of per-share dividend amounts in the daily price history over the fiscal year's window `(Pᵢ₋₁, Pᵢ]` — one source for all dividend figures; the 0.1 cash-flow ÷ shares-outstanding figure (which drifts with buybacks) is dropped |
| TTM dividends per share | sum of per-share dividend amounts in the 365 days up to `as_of` |
| Dividend yield | TTM dividends per share / present price |
| Cash Position totals (D18) | total cash = cash + short-term investments; net cash = total cash − long-term debt; working capital = current assets − current liabilities; per-share figures divide by shares outstanding. Missing summed items count as 0 (`assumed_zero`). |
| **Lynch dividend-adjusted ratio** (D15) | **Peter Lynch's dividend-adjusted PEG** (*One Up on Wall Street*, 1989): `(EPS growth % + dividend yield %) / current PE`, where both percentages are whole numbers. EPS growth is the EPS CAGR (§ Past EPS). Example: 12 % growth, 2 % yield, PE 20 → (12 + 2) / 20 = **0.7**. Lynch's reading: below 1 poor, 1.5 okay, 2 or more what you're looking for. `null` if PE or EPS CAGR is unavailable. Replaces 0.1's `((avg revenue growth × 10) + dividends per share) / PE`, which mixed units. |

**Where Lynch's ratio appears:** `research/definitions.py` (`lynch_ratio`), the `revenue_growth`
section result and its JSON field `lynch_ratio`, the Revenue Growth block of the Discord and text
reports (labelled "Lynch dividend-adjusted ratio"), `CONTEXT.md`, and the README's calculation notes.

## 6. Rendering

* **Text** renderers per section live in `render/text.py`; **Discord** wraps them with Discord markdown
  and 2000-character chunking. Both show `FY####` labels, warnings, and figure labels from §5.7.
* **JSON** (§8.2) serialises `to_dict()`. Never contains images.
* **Charts** use matplotlib's object-oriented `Figure` API (never the global `pyplot` state, which is not
  thread-safe), close every figure, and write PNGs only into a directory the caller supplies: a
  per-request temporary directory in the bot, `--charts DIR` in the CLI.

## 7. Caching

* The raw yfinance responses for a ticker are cached as one recording in
  `$STOCKYARD_DATA_DIR/cache/<TICKER>.json`, where `<TICKER>` has passed `validate_ticker` (no path
  traversal). A miss records the real calls; a hit replays them (`stockyard.data.cache`, the same
  mechanism the test fixtures use).
* An entry is valid for the **America/New_York calendar day** it was fetched. A fetch during the
  trading day may include a partial last bar; Present price reflects it, and the report's `as_of`
  states the day.
* Format: JSON with timezones preserved, never pickle. Writes are atomic (temp file + rename).
* Empty or failed fetches are never cached.
* `--no-cache` bypasses reads and writes.

### 8.2 JSON contract

```json
{
  "schema": "stockyard.report/1",
  "ticker": "MSFT",
  "as_of": "2026-11-11",
  "source": "yfinance",
  "history": {"complete_fiscal_years": 5, "quarters_reported": 1, "price_history_start": "2021-07-01", "sufficient_history": true},
  "warnings": [{"code": "figure_unavailable", "section": "risk_reward", "figure": "forecast_high", "message": "...", "needed": 5, "available": 3}],
  "sections": {"past_eps": {"...": "..."}, "risk_reward": null}
}
```

* Single-section commands emit the same envelope with one key in `sections`.
* A failed section is present with value `null` and a `section_failed` warning.
* Errors with `--json` (exit 3, 4, 1) emit `{"schema": "stockyard.error/1", "code": ..., "message": ...}`.
* Dates ISO-8601; fiscal years as `"fiscal_year": "FY2025"` plus `"period_end"`. `null` for missing,
  `NaN` or `±∞`. No `currency` field in v1 (no source supplies it without an extra slow call).
* **Compatibility:** adding fields is non-breaking; renaming, removing or changing the meaning of a field
  bumps to `/2`. A snapshot test pins the key set of the envelope **and of every section's `to_dict()`**.

### 8.3 Discord bot (Warren)

* Commands: `!stock_report`/`!sr TICKER`, `!help`. The `!bug` stub is removed.
* Report generation runs in `asyncio.to_thread`; chart rendering is thread-safe (§6).
* The token is resolved in `main()`, not at import.

## 11. Testing strategy

Strict TDD per `CLAUDE.md`.

* **Definitions (§5.7):** each formula in `research/definitions.py` has hand-computed tests, including
  every `null` rule. Lynch's ratio is pinned with the 12 / 2 / 20 → 0.7 example and a units test that
  fails if fractions are passed where whole-number percentages belong.
* **Alignment (§5.2):** synthetic frames for each rule (Dec and non-Dec year ends, 52/53-week ends,
  late, duplicate and unattributable announcements, 3-of-4 quarters, 4-of-4 awaiting the annual
  statement, pre-IPO years with revenue but no EPS, ≥ 5 vs < 5 complete years).
* **Recorded fixtures:** IBM (existing), a non-December filer (MSFT, FY ends 30 June), a recent IPO.
  Dates are no longer shifted; tests pin `as_of` to the fixture's snapshot date.
* **Architecture:** walks every submodule of `stockyard.data` and `stockyard.research`.
* **JSON:** key-set snapshots of the envelope and every section.
* **Charts:** concurrent rendering from threads produces correct files and leaves no open figures.
* **CLI / bot:** in-process with fakes; no network anywhere in the suite.
